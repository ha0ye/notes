### 5. Please share your approach to managing your time, especially when you are faced with competing priorities. How do you approach managing multiple, complex, high-profile projects?

3 main considerations
* fixed deadlines (paper, abstract, report submissions)
* for group projects, extent to which my component might be blocking or facilitate someone else
* kind of work (complexity, deep vs. superficial) and how much time I have / time of day - context switching and energy for creative work

Tracking
* digital notes (copied and updated every week)
* post-its for more immediate reminders
* white board to keep track of longer-time deadlines

Practices
* planner by nature
* also impatient
* prototype and try things
  - commit to not getting everything perfect the first time
  - iteration and improvement will be necessary
* reflect and evaluate quickly to adjust
* assess possibility of issues and effort to correct
