6.  What is your communication preference – email, meetings (zoom or in-person), drop-by conversations?

depends entirely on the purpose:
* synchronous channels (meetings / conversations) - good for resolving ambiguitiy, shared thinking and decision-making, high-priority work (or scheduling co-working time)
* asynchronous channels (email, etc.) - for reminders and transient information, email is good. If information needs to be persistent, then a shared document space (e.g. Teams, Google Docs, GitHub) is better for the permenant record
