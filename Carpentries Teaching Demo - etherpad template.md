## 2. Teaching Demo Procedures

#### Checks:
- Are signed up participants present?
- Are participants prepared to teach 5 minutes from any episode within their chosen lessons?

#### Procedures:
__**The order of teaching demos has been randomly determined.**__

On your turn:
- I will tell you what episode you will be teaching from.
- You will have time to set up what you need to teach that episode.
- You will start screen sharing. (any questions about screen sharing?)
- I recommend sharing just the application unless you want to be able to show something from a different application.
- You are welcome to keep any notes/websites open in another window/device for reference, just as though you were teaching.
- You will have 5 minutes for your demo.
- I will tell you when to start.
- I will use https://cuckoo.team/carpentries-teaching-demo to provide a shared timer - you are welcome to leave this open in a separate window.
- I will let you know when your time is up.
- I will review feedback.

**After all teaching demos are done, I will review next steps. You are welcome to ask any questions about this or instructor training checkout.**
