* open science is not different from traditional science - more transparent and open
* open science is an umbrella term
* open science closely related to FAIR
* myths:
  - sharing compromises privacy
  - fears of getting scooped
  - no credit for sharing
* open science integration into the research life cycle
* open science: five schools of thought (book chapter, Fecher and Friesike 2014)
  - pragmatic (cooperation -> efficient)
  - democratic (knowledge should be freely available)
  - public (lay people should have access)
  - measurement (broader measures of contributions)
  - infrastructure (sharing tools -> efficiency)
