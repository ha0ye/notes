## Basic Demo Outline

0. Email participants 1-2 days ahead of time
1. Welcome, reminder for Code of Conduct
2. Check participants
  - are all the participants who signed up on the etherpad present?
  - if there are missing, then can accommodate drop-ins
3. Are the participants prepared for their selected lesson?
4. Review usage of etherpad and structure
  - other participants record written feedback
6. Feedback instructions
  - shared timer for demo givers - https://cuckoo.team/carpentries-teaching-demo
  - review rubric
7. For each demo
  - select an episode - https://carpentries.github.io/instructor-training/demo_lessons/index.html
  - proceed with demo
  - summarize feedback and give feedback
  - fill out rubric - https://carpentries.github.io/instructor-training/demos_rubric/
8. After all demos
  - Ask for general questions
  - If all of your trainees passed, you can tell the group at the end of the demo session. If anyone did not pass, tell everyone you will send them each an email to let them know if they passed.
9. Submit form after session - https://forms.gle/ZusNhyhNh4rvCmxH8
