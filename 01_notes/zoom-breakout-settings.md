## Recommended settings:

Make sure to update zoom, some breakout features are restricted to newer versions.

Zoom Application:
* General -> CHECK "Always show meeting controls"
* Video -> UNCHECK "Hide non-video participants" (so you have a visual indicator of people present)

Online Profile:
* Meetings -> CHECK "Allow participants to rename themselves" (in case you are asking folks to include pronouns or indicate which room to be assigned into)
* Meetings -> CHECK "Breakout room"
* Meetings -> CHECK "Allow host to assign participants to breakout rooms when scheduling"
* Meetings -> CHECK "Allow host to broadcast message to participants"
* Meetings -> CHECK "Allow the host to add co-hosts. Co-hosts have the same in-meeting controls as the host." (to let co-hosts manage breakout rooms)

## Instructions

Pre-Creating Breakout Rooms:
* When setting up a meeting, click "Show" next to "Options"
  - "Check Breakout Room pre-assign"
  - You can create rooms.
  - (OPTIONAL) You can assign people by email address (but they may not be pre-assigned if they don't join the meeting using that email; e.g. signing in as guest instead of with UF SSO)

Breakouts during a meeting:
* Starting breakouts
  - Click on "Breakout Rooms"
  - select options accordingly
  - Click "Open All Rooms"
* Assign or moving people
  - Click on "Breakout Rooms"
  - select options accordingly
* NOTES
  - if you pre-set rooms or pre-assign participants, Zoom defaults to NOT allowing participants to select their own room!
    + FIX: Before "Open All Rooms", click on the settings icon (looks like a gear)
    + Check settings, e.g. "Allow participants to choose room"
