Adithya Gopinath: CNS-to-Periphery Neuroimmune Communication. (Neuroscience; Mentor: Khoshbouie)

In Parkinson's disease, brain dopamine depletion triggers changes in peripheral monocytes

* peripheral immune cells are influenced by dopamine
  - some tissues in the body encounter high amounts of tissue and CNS dopamine
* monocytes express dopamine machinery proteins
  - TH: rate-limiting enzyme in dopamine synthesis
  - DAT: 
* Parkinson's
  - reduced brain dopamine; increased inflammatory mediators
  - peripheral immune system
* Hypothesis: monocytes can be exposed to dopamine and inflammatory mediators; thus exhibit response to Parkinson's conditions

  - healthy patients (n = 62): ~15% express TH / DAT
  - drug naive (n = 12): ~26% express TH / DAT
  - treated (n = 84): ~ 20% express TH / DAT (more similar to health patients)

- difference between TH and DAT expression, is it meaningful that expression levels are similar

- no increase in TH and DAT in alzheimers, relative to controls
