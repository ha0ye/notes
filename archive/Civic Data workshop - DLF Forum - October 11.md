## Quick Write: Your Library and Open Civic Data

Q: What **civic data role(s)** can you imagine for your library?

* assemble and aggregate sources of civic data
* identify non-civic data that could be relevant for use in conjunction with civic data; what are their access restrictions? (e.g. private or individual health data)
* map out local organizations and experts
  - non-profit or mission-driven civic organizations
  - university domain-specific experts
  - data and coding groups
  - funding sources
  - stakeholders
  - research experts?
  - educators or other individuals bringing in relevant skills/expertise
  - journalists / publicists 
  - data producers (government agencies, university programs - IFAS, Florida Museum, local organizations)
* provide expertise on working with data, including data management
